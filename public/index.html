The Core Framework

The 4D Framework for AI Fluency

Developed by Prof. Joseph Feller (UCC) and Prof. Rick Dakan (Ringling College) in partnership with Anthropic. This is the organising structure behind everything we teach — and Anthropic's own AI Fluency Index research validates it across thousands of real conversations.

D

Delegation

Deciding what tasks to give to AI — and which to keep. Understanding automation, augmentation, and agency.

D

Description

Providing clarity and context — the problem, desired outcomes, approach, and behaviour — to maximise effectiveness.

D

Discernment

Evaluating AI output for accuracy, reasoning, alignment with goals, and fitness for purpose. The critical human skill.

D

Diligence

Ensuring AI use avoids issues of accuracy, bias, privacy, and ethical risk. The responsibility layer.

Why this matters: Anthropic's AI Fluency Index research found that 85.7% of effective Claude conversations exhibit iteration and refinement — and those conversations show substantially higher rates of all four 4D behaviours. The framework isn't theory; it's validated by real usage data.
